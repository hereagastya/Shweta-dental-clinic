# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step, no backend). The user wanted whatever "looks best as a real-world clinic site"; a hand-built static site gives full control over typography and motion, loads fast on mobile, and deploys anywhere. Forms are front-end only and simulate submission.

## Users

Affluent adults in Mumbai's western suburbs (Bandra, Khar, Santacruz, Juhu) who want cosmetic and restorative work: veneers, clear aligners, whitening, implants, smile makeovers. Most arrive on a phone, often from Instagram or a Google Maps search, and decide in under a minute whether this clinic feels trustworthy and premium enough to book.

## Product Purpose

Website for Dr. Shweta Dental Clinic, led by Dr. Shweta Tiwari. The site must make a first-time visitor trust the dentist, understand what she offers, and book a consultation (form, call or WhatsApp). Used as a pitch to the client, so it must read as a real, established clinic's site, not a demo.

## Positioning

A single-dentist practice where Dr. Shweta Tiwari personally plans every smile: unhurried appointments, digital smile design before any work, conservative cosmetic dentistry.

- Must look best on mobile.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. With the user's approval, credentials, years in practice, address, hours, indicative fees and patient reviews are AUTHORED and must be listed for the user to replace before going live.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: credentials, process and real-feeling specifics outrank slogans.
2. One tap to book from anywhere on the page, especially on mobile.
3. Calm and unhurried: the site should feel like the appointment does.
4. Nothing should read as a template or placeholder.
