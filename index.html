Questions patients ask

Will it hurt?

Very rarely. We use a computer-controlled anaesthetic that numbs slowly and quietly, and most cosmetic work needs little or none. If you're anxious, tell us when you book and we'll schedule a longer, gentler appointment.

How much is a consultation?

₹1,000 for a 45-minute consultation with photographs and a 3D scan. If you go ahead with treatment, it's adjusted against your fee.

Do you offer EMI?

Yes. 0% EMI over 6 months on treatments above ₹30,000, through our finance partner. We also take all cards and UPI.

I have a dental emergency. Can you see me today?

We keep two emergency slots open every day, at 1:30 pm and 8 pm. Call or WhatsApp +91 72985 12186 and we'll fit you in.

Where do I park?

There's street parking on 33rd Road, and the Linking Road pay-and-park is a three-minute walk. From Bandra station it's about ten minutes by auto.

Do you see children?

Yes, from age six, for check-ups, cleanings, fillings and early orthodontic assessments.
